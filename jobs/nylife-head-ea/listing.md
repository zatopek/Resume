Role Overview

The Head of Enterprise Architecture (EA) will be part of the Enterprise Technology Services team, reporting directly to the Chief Technology Officer. EA serves as a strategic partner and advisor to both business and technology teams, offering proactive, holistic support to help achieve their goals. The ideal candidate is an experienced technologist with years of practical experience engineering and developing enterprise technology solutions. Each day in the role is an opportunity to drive outcomes in the form of industry leading digital client experiences. Collaboration and developing partnerships are a critical success factory. You will build and support the EA Domain Architect Community who will leverage their collective experience to build a comprehensive portfolio of enterprise capabilities consumed by our diverse portfolio of insurance, investments, and benefits products & services. The ideal candidate must focus on facilitating technology solutions keeping agility in balance with governance and standards. The focus of the role is building reuseable patterns for enterprise consumption creating a fast path for delivery of capabilities the broader technology organization. EA plays a key role in guiding the adoption of new and emerging technologies to meet business needs. As we adopt new technologies the EA community maintains a technology roadmap which addresses lifecycle management cost efficiency within our software portfolio.
