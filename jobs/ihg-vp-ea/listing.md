About the job
The Role

The Vice President of Enterprise Architecture (EA) is a strategic leadership role responsible for defining and advancing the technology architecture vision across a global hospitality organization. This leader ensures alignment between business strategy and technology capabilities, driving innovation, scalability, and operational excellence across brand, guest experience, property operations, and corporate functions. The VP, Enterprise Architecture will lead the design and governance of enterprise-wide architecture frameworks, enabling seamless integration across property management systems (PMS), customer platforms, loyalty systems, digital channels, and back-office operations.

Your Day-to-Day

Strategic Leadership

Define and execute the enterprise architecture strategy aligned to business priorities including guest experience, revenue growth, and operational efficiency.
Serve as a trusted advisor to executive leadership on technology trends, digital transformation, and innovation opportunities.
Establish and evolve architecture frameworks, standards, and governance processes across all business domains.

Business & Technology Alignment

Partner with business leaders across hotel operations, marketing, loyalty, revenue management, and finance to enable technology-driven outcomes.
Translate business capabilities into scalable architecture blueprints and roadmaps.
Ensure alignment of IT strategy with enterprise-wide digital transformation initiatives.
Lead Enterprise AI Transformation Through a Forward-Deployed Architecture Model

Define and manage enterprise architecture domains, including:

Data & Digital Innovation
