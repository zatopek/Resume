Company Description

AllCare is a fast-growing, tech-enabled provider of patient-centered care, built to address a fundamental challenge in healthcare: access. Too often, patients struggle to receive timely, coordinated primary care, leading them into more expensive, fragmented settings and ultimately poorer outcomes.

At our core, our mission is to modernize the healthcare experience with care that is accessible, connected, and human, delivered through an omnichannel model that integrates primary and urgent care—combining digital scheduling, walk-in access, and telehealth into a modern patient experience. Our vision is a future where patients never have to choose between convenience, compassion, and continuity; by bringing these elements together under one model, AllCare is redefining how care is delivered—ensuring patients receive timely access without sacrificing quality relationships or long-term care.

Central to our strategy is a commitment to being technology-forward and AI-enabled. We are investing in platforms and capabilities that not only improve the patient experience, but also power a highly efficient and scalable operating model. This includes leveraging AI and automation to streamline administrative workflows, reduce friction in the back office, and enable our clinical teams to focus more of their time on patient care. The result is a system that delivers both better outcomes and stronger unit economics as we scale.

Currently serving patients across 15 locations in Georgia, AllCare offers a full spectrum of services including preventive care, women’s health, and chronic disease management. Our clinical and operating model is intentionally designed to support providers—offering flexible scheduling, strong administrative infrastructure, and a collaborative team environment.

Following an investment by Summit Partners, AllCare is poised for significant growth, with plans to scale from 15 to 50+ locations over the next several years, while staying true to our mission of delivering high-quality, convenient, and human-centered care.
